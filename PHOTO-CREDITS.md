# Category image usage
Existing Spider's Creations images are used for most categories. Some category tiles use external, royalty-free Unsplash photography for **inspiration**, not photography of products made by Spider's Creations. Replace inspiration with your own finished-product photographs when available. Their use does not imply affiliation with the photographers or with any other vendor.

Photo sources: [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license). Photo selections are referenced in `js/category-images.js`. No artwork is represented as customer orders unless independently confirmed.
