FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

How do I order something?

Start with All Products or Design Library. Open an item, press Customize, choose your details, save it to your quote basket, then submit the project request form. We reply with pricing and next steps.

Can I change the text, font, color, size or material?

Yes. The customization pages let you describe preferred materials, sizes, names, fonts, colors, quantity and other design requirements. Availability and the production method are confirmed before the order is accepted.

Can you make something that isn't listed?

Yes, you can send a custom project request with artwork, a photo, size, material and quantity. We'll review whether it can be made and provide a quote.

Can I upload a picture or logo?

The project request form accepts optional reference artwork, subject to file size and format limits. Please provide only images and artwork you have permission to use.

Where are the sports, anime, fishing and floral tumbler designs?

Open the Design Library, then select a collection, category or team. You can search by text and save designs you like. Saved pictures are concept previews; production artwork is reviewed separately.

Why don't all products have a checkout price?

Most products are custom made. Material, size, artwork, setup, quantity and deadline affect the price. Your basket is for quote requests; we do not charge credit cards or promise inventory online.

How do I change or remove something in my basket?

Open My Quote Basket. You can remove saved designs and edit compatible requests. Information saved in your browser may be lost if you clear browser data or switch devices.

How long does production or shipping take?

We confirm timing during the quote process. Include the date you need your product; do not assume a delivery date before we confirm it.

Can I request team or branded artwork?

You can describe the style or team you're interested in. Protected logos and copyrighted artwork can only be produced when appropriate permission or licensing exists. The website is not affiliated with professional sports leagues.
